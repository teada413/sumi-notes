Sumi Notes Version History

The changes in each version of Sumi Notes, newest first. The dates are when each version was released on Microsoft Store.

  • 1.1.0
  • 1.0.3
  • 1.0.1

1.1.0

Released: November 12, 2026

  • Sumi Notes is now available in English. On first launch, it shows Japanese or English to match your Windows display language, and you can switch under Language in Settings.

1.0.3

Released: November 9, 2026

  • In the "Editor Only" and "Preview Only" views, the text now widens to fit the window. The "Side by Side" view is unchanged.
  • Fixed a bug where, with several lines selected, the selection color on some lines did not start at the first character.

1.0.1

Released: November 2, 2026

  • Released on Microsoft Store as the first version.
